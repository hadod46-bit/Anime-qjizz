How to play
  1. Qualifying round: first to 9 points. Buzz: the clock stops for everyone and 4 choices appear for you. Questions are worth 1, then 2, then 3 points. The first players to reach 9 go through: with 2 players, straight to the final; with 3, the first 2 go to the final; with 4, the first 3 go to the semi-final; with 5 or more, the first 4.
  2. Semi-final: 4 in a row. One player at a time, 60 seconds to type 4 right answers in a row. A mistake or a skip resets your streak. Your score is your best streak. The best 2 go to the final.
  3. Final: head to head. The pictures appear one by one. Buzz early: 4 pts with 1 picture, 3 pts with 2, 2 pts with 3, 1 pt with 4. The first to the target score is crowned champion!
  4. Bonus: the first to qualify starts the final with +1 point, the semi-final winner with +2.

📱 Online: one player creates a room and sends the code (or the link) to the others (up to 8 players). Everyone buzzes with the big button on their phone.

🏷️ Logo quiz: a brand logo appears blurred and zoomed in, then gets sharper every 5 seconds. Buzzing early is harder… but in the final it scores more!

🎤 Blind test: hits from the 70s to today (pop, rap, rock, R&B, electronic, French songs) and Moroccan chaabi 🇲🇦. Find the title or the artist. 30-second previews.

🎵 Anime music quiz: an anime opening or ending plays instead of the pictures, with 10 extra seconds on every clock. A buzz stops the music for everyone.

📺 Same screen: every player has a buzzer at the bottom of the screen (or the keys A, P, V, M). Keys 1-4 answer the multiple choice, Enter moves on.

A typed answer was refused even though it was right? The “I was right!” button counts it.
