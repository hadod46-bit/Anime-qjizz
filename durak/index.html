How to play Durak
  1. Goal: get rid of all your cards. The last player holding cards is the Durak (“the fool”)!
  2. 36 cards (6 up to Ace). Everyone gets 6. The card turned face up under the deck sets the trump suit, and it is the last card to be drawn.
  3. First game: the player with the lowest trump attacks the player on their left. Next games: the Durak deals, and the player before them attacks them first.
  4. Defending: beat each attacking card with a higher card of the same suit, or with any trump (a trump can only be beaten by a higher trump).
  5. Throwing in: every attacker may add cards of a rank already on the table: at most 5 in the first bout of a game, 6 after that, and never more than the defender can answer.
  6. Everything beaten: “Bito!” The cards are discarded and the defender attacks next.
  7. Can't (or won't) defend: pick up all the cards (the others may still throw in) and lose your turn to attack.
  8. After each bout, everyone draws back up to 6 cards: main attacker first, defender last.
  9. When the deck is empty, players without cards are out. The last one left is the Durak. If the last players go out together, it's a draw.
  10. Perevodnoy (transfer) version: before beating anything, the defender may play a card of the same rank to pass the whole attack to the next player, if that player has enough cards to answer it. The new defender can pass it on again!

Online: 2 to 6 players. The host can add bots 🤖 to fill the table. If someone's phone disconnects, a bot plays for them until they're back.
